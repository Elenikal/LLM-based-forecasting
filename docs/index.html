Reading, not forecasting. Claude reads each set of FOMC minutes and each Beige Book and scores five dimensions (growth, inflation concern, labor market, credit conditions, policy uncertainty) from −2 to +2, treating the document as undated. Three rolling principal components of the ten scores enter an AR(4) and a Bayesian VAR as exogenous regressors. All numerical prediction is done by the time-series models.

Backtest. 20 rolling origins, 2020Q1–2024Q4, 40-quarter windows. The h-step forecast is scored against the value at t+h−1. The March 2026 version of the paper scored both horizons against the value at t; the corrected results are shown here and the originals are kept in outputs/paper_v1_march2026/.

Live record. Issued once per FOMC cycle, before the decision. Each forecast file is committed and tagged on GitHub before any outcome is known, and is never edited afterwards. Outcomes are first-release values (ALFRED vintages), not revised data.

  • Live specification: the paper's AR(4) and AR(4)+LLM, plus impulse dummies for 2020Q2–Q4, declared before the first issue. Under that specification the backtest gives 9 of 12 wins.
  • Headline variables (declared 2026-10-14): CPI, core PCE, unemployment, fed funds and the 10Y–2Y spread. GDP is still forecast and graded, but not headlined.
  • Scorer: claude-sonnet-4-5, temperature 0.3, three runs per document. The model retires on 2026-11-30; the switch to its successor will be logged in the repository.
  • Outside forecasts (SPF, GDPNow, Cleveland Fed nowcast, fed funds futures) are recorded on the issue date with their definitions and graded against the same first releases.
